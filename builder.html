Offline backup video

In case there's no internet at the venue, this renders the slideshow into a regular 1920x1080 video file (title card to closing card) you can save to a laptop or USB drive as a backup. Rendering happens in this tab and is usually much faster than the show's actual runtime, but can still take a few minutes for a lot of photos/video — leave the tab open until it finishes and downloads automatically. Test the downloaded file plays on whatever device you'll actually use as backup, ahead of time.
